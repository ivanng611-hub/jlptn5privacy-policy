1. Introduction

Welcome to JLPT N5 Japanese Study ("the App", "we", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy is straightforward because we do not collect, store, or process any personal data from our users.

✅

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

✓ The App does not collect any personal information whatsoever.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data
  • Usage statistics, analytics, or behavioural data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data sent to our servers
  • Payment information or financial data
  • Photos, camera, microphone, or any device sensor data
  • Contacts, calendar, or any other device content
⚙️

3. How the App Works

JLPT N5 Japanese Study is a fully offline application. All learning content — including vocabulary, grammar patterns, reading passages, and quiz questions — is bundled directly within the App and stored locally on your device.

The App does not communicate with any external server, cloud service, or third-party API. No data leaves your device at any time.

  • Vocabulary: 800 N5 words stored locally
  • Grammar: 50 patterns stored locally
  • Reading: 10 passages stored locally
  • Quiz: 100 questions stored locally
🌐

4. Internet Permission

The App requests the INTERNET permission in its Android manifest. This permission is required by the underlying WebView technology (Capacitor) used to render the App's interface.

However, the App makes no outbound network requests. The permission is declared as a technical requirement of the framework and is not used to transmit any data.

🔗

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services, including:

  • Analytics platforms (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics)
  • Advertising networks (e.g. AdMob, Meta Audience Network)
  • Crash reporting tools (e.g. Crashlytics, Sentry)
  • Social media SDKs (e.g. Facebook, Twitter)
  • Cloud storage or database services

The only third-party component used is Capacitor (an open-source framework by Ionic), which is used solely to package the web application as a native Android app. Capacitor does not collect or transmit user data.

💾

6. Local Data Storage

The App may store minimal data locally on your device, such as your selected display language (Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, or English) and the current quiz session state. This data:

  • Is stored only on your device
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the App
  • Contains no personally identifiable information
👶
